<p>Run compatible GGUF language models on your Android phone, or connect a remote provider. Import a model from storage or download it from Hugging Face, with an optional access token. No models are bundled.</p>
<ul>
<li>Messenger-style chats with folders, search, pinned messages and a model information card.</li>
<li>Local CPU inference, adjustable context and answer budgets, and per-model profiles. The original model weights are preserved.</li>
<li>Optional OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints, with streaming replies and model catalogs.</li>
<li>HTTP endpoints require explicit consent and remain visibly marked as unencrypted. Use HTTPS for confidential traffic.</li>
<li>Manual context compaction and optional automatic compaction, off by default. Inspect or undo the summary; the original history follows the chat's storage mode.</li>
<li>Temporary chats, opt-in encrypted persistent history, retention settings and automatic deletion. Android may delay scheduled cleanup.</li>
<li>Background replies with completion notifications. Android power management may interrupt background work.</li>
<li>An optional OpenAI-compatible local API with streaming. Loopback requires a bearer token; LAN access requires both TLS and a token.</li>
<li>Model-supported image/PDF attachments and downloadable provider outputs. Support depends on the selected model and provider.</li>
<li>English and Russian UI, light and dark themes. Conversation languages depend on the model.</li>
</ul>
<p>Local chat works offline after downloading a model. No telemetry, mandatory account, root or Google Play Services. Remote requests and attachments are sent to the provider you select; its terms, charges and retention policy apply. Model licenses and access rights are separate from the app.</p>
<p>Requires Android 9 or later and a 64-bit ARM64 OS. Physically tested on Motorola Edge 40 Pro with 12 GB RAM using pinned Ornith and Gemma GGUF files; see the repository for exact models and measurements. Other devices and arbitrary GGUF files are not guaranteed. Large models and contexts can be slow or exceed available memory. GPU experiments are disabled in this build.</p>
